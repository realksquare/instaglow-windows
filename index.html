Glazing Technology

Engineered for Ultimate Warmth & Efficiency

Discover the multi-layered science behind our high-performance double and triple-glazed units.

01

Two Glass Panes with Sealed Gap

Precision-engineered dual glass panes separated by an airtight sealed cavity that forms a resilient thermal and acoustic sound barrier.

02

Argon Gas Filled Cavity

Dense, harmless argon gas sealed within the gap acts as a superior insulator, significantly slowing down heat transfer compared to air.

03

Low-Emissivity (Low-E) Glass

A microscopic metallic coating that reflects radiant interior heat back into your home while letting abundant natural daylight flood in.

04

Insulated Frames & Warm-Edge Spacers

Composite thermal spacers and multi-chambered insulated frames minimize edge conduction, virtually eliminating perimeter heat loss.

The All-Season Home Comfort Advantage

Together, these features keep more warmth indoors during winter, limit unwanted heat in summer, reduce condensation and outside noise, and deliver lasting lower energy bills for your home.
